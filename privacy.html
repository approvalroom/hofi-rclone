Privacy Policy

HOFI rclone is used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's Google Drive solely to upload, verify, and restore encrypted backup files created by the HOFI server. Data is encrypted before upload, is not shared with or sold to any third party, and is not used for advertising. Google user data is used only for this backup purpose, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

The owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
